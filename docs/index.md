# AI Resources for MSBA Students

This site collects the podcasts, people, tools, courses, and readings that we recommend to students in the Master of Science in Business Analytics (MSBA) program who want to keep pace with the rapidly changing world of artificial intelligence (AI). Coursework in an academic program moves slowly by design, whereas the tools that employers expect analysts to use change every few months. Thus, the site serves as a supplement to the curriculum rather than a replacement for it, and it is organized around two entry points. The [learning path](path/index.md) sequences a set of biweekly enrichment modules across the semester, each of which points at a small number of specific resources. The catalog lists every recommended resource by type (see [Podcasts](catalog/podcasts.md), [People and Newsletters](catalog/people.md), [Tools with Student Offers](catalog/tools.md), [Courses](catalog/courses.md), and [Readings](catalog/readings.md)), so that a student who wants a podcast for the commute or a newsletter for the weekend can find one without working through the modules in order.

Every entry on this site has been verified before publication. Verification refers to a three-part check in which (i) the link was fetched and found live, (ii) every factual claim in the annotation (e.g., a student discount, a price, or a release date) was matched against a primary source such as the vendor's own page, and (iii) a second, adversarial review attempted to refute the entry and failed. The date shown as "last verified" on each entry is the date of the most recent such check. An offer that has changed since that date may no longer be available, and we encourage students to confirm the terms on the vendor page before relying on them. The full verification record, including the sources consulted and the reasons for each decision, is kept in the [public repository](https://github.com/nkfreeman-teaching/ai-resources) that generates this site.
