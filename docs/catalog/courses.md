# Courses

The courses listed here are structured, multi-session materials (e.g., a video series with exercises or a free online course) that we consider worth a student's time. Each entry states who teaches the course, what it covers, and whether it is free.

<!-- resources: type=course -->
### [ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/chatgpt-prompt-eng/)

**Course**, DeepLearning.AI (with OpenAI). Nine video lessons on effective prompting and on using large language models to summarize, infer, transform, and expand text, free for a limited time during the learning platform beta. Featured in [Module 2](../path/module-02.md), [Module 3](../path/module-03.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*

### [Claude 101 (Anthropic Academy)](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-101)

**Course**, Anthropic. Thirteen lessons and one quiz taking about 2.5 hours to complete, marked free to access in the course page's structured data, which gives students a first structured pass at working with Claude. Featured in [Module 2](../path/module-02.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*

### [Generative AI for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners)

**Course**, Microsoft Cloud Advocates. Twenty-one lessons on building generative AI applications, each covering its own topic and open to study in any order, beginning with what generative AI is and how large language models work. Featured in [Module 5](../path/module-05.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*

### [OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/pages/courses)

**Course**, OpenAI. Sign-up is free and open to anyone, and the courses teach practical AI skills through hands-on learning about how AI works and how to build agents and workflows, with course completion certificates available. Featured in [Module 5](../path/module-05.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*
