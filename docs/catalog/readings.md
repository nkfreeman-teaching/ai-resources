# Readings

The readings listed here are individual articles, guides, or books that explain a specific concept well enough to be assigned. Each entry states the author, the concept covered, and the date on which we last confirmed the link.

<!-- resources: type=reading -->
### [Best Practices for Prompt Engineering for 2026 (Claude)](https://claude.com/blog/best-practices-for-prompt-engineering)

**Reading**, Anthropic. Guidance on telling Claude exactly what output is wanted, stating what that output should include rather than only what task to perform, and asking explicitly when a full and detailed answer is needed. Featured in [Module 2](../path/module-02.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*

### [Prompting Guide 101: Gemini for Google Workspace (October 2024 edition)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/gemini_for_workspace_prompt_guide_october_2024_digital_final.pdf)

**Reading**, Google Workspace. Advice on writing prompts in natural language as complete sentences addressed to another person, noting that the most fruitful prompts average around 21 words of relevant context while typical attempts run under nine. Featured in [Module 2](../path/module-02.md), [Module 3](../path/module-03.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*

### [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/)

**Reading**, DeepLearning.AI. A publication covering what matters in AI right now, delivering weekly AI news and insights by email to students who prefer an edited summary over a continuous feed. Featured in [Module 6](../path/module-06.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*

### [TLDR AI](https://tldr.tech/ai)

**Reading**, TLDR. A free daily email sent every weekday covering the most interesting AI stories and breakthroughs, which gives students a short regular scan of developments at no cost. Featured in [Module 6](../path/module-06.md). *Last verified 2026-09-07.*
